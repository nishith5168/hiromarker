# The Avocado Gallery – AR Mini Museum
## How to use

1. Open the hosted link on your phone (HTTPS is required for camera access).
2. Allow camera access.
3. Point the camera at the Hiro marker.
4. Tap the **floating ball** to change its colour.
5. Tap the **orange button** to show or hide the exhibit information.
